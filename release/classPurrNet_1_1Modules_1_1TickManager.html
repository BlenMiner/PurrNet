double rtt [get]
 Round trip time in seconds, measured by the tick-sync ping: local time from sending the request to receiving the server's response. The ping bypasses tick batching on both ends (immediate receive, same-frame send flush), so this is network round trip plus at most a frame of scheduling on each side rather than the tick interval.
